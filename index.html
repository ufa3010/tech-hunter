Tech Hunter

A personal tool that looks for free, cheap and broken computer hardware in Switzerland.

Tech Hunter is run by Youssef Riad for his own use. It is not a public service and you cannot sign up for it.

What it does

  • Reads marketplace alert emails in its own Gmail inbox and sorts the listings by price, distance and condition.
  • Helps write emails to schools, companies and other organisations that may give away old hardware.
  • Sends an email only after its owner approves it. If someone replies "no" or "stop", it never contacts them again.
