Tech Hunter

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Tech Hunter is a personal tool run by Youssef Riad (contact: youssef.riad.swiss@gmail.com). It only connects to one Google account, its own inbox youssef.riad.swiss@gmail.com.

Google data it uses

  • Read access (gmail.readonly): to read marketplace alert emails and replies from organisations it has written to.
  • Send access (gmail.send): to send emails that its owner approved, and short thank-you notes in conversations he already approved.

Tech Hunter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the data goes

  • Email content and listings are stored on the owner's own computer in Switzerland.
  • Short pieces of text are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to sort listings and draft replies. This data is not used to train AI models.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else. This website has no cookies or tracking.

If Tech Hunter emailed you

It keeps your organisation's name, your email address and the conversation so it does not write to you twice. Reply "stop" and it will never contact you again. Email youssef.riad.swiss@gmail.com to have your data deleted.

Revoking access

The account owner can remove Tech Hunter's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
